Schengen visa from Pakistan

How to apply for a short-stay visa to travel across Europe’s Schengen countries.

Who needs it

Pakistani passport holders need a Schengen visa for short trips to countries such as France, Germany, Italy, Spain and others. A short-stay visa allows up to 90 days in any 180-day period. You apply to the country where you will spend the most nights, or the country of first entry if the stay is equal.

Documents you usually need

  • Passport valid for at least 3 months after you plan to leave, with blank pages
  • Completed application form and recent photos
  • Travel health insurance that covers Schengen countries
  • Flight reservation and hotel booking or host invitation
  • Bank statements, income proof and employment or business papers
  • Proof of your ties to Pakistan
  • Cover letter explaining your trip

How to apply

  1. Choose the right embassy: the country where you spend the most nights.
  2. Check that country’s official site for the visa centre and appointment steps.
  3. Book an appointment, usually through its visa application centre.
  4. Submit your documents and fingerprints at the appointment.
  5. Wait for the decision. Do not buy non-refundable tickets before approval.

Tips

  • Apply early, as appointments can be limited in busy seasons.
  • Make sure your dates, bookings and insurance all match.
  • Show clear finances and a clear reason to return home.
  • Never use fake documents. A refusal for this can affect future applications.

Official links
