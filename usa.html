US visitor visa from Pakistan

How the B1/B2 tourist and business visa process works, from the online form to the interview.

Who needs it

Pakistani passport holders need a visa before travelling to the USA for tourism, family visits or business. Pakistan is not part of the Visa Waiver Program, so there is no ESTA option. Tourism and family visits use B2, and business visits use B1. Most applicants get a combined B1/B2.

Documents you usually need

  • Valid passport
  • DS-160 confirmation page with barcode
  • Visa fee payment receipt
  • One recent photo that meets US photo rules
  • Proof of your ties to Pakistan, such as job letter, business papers, family and property documents
  • Recent bank statements and income proof
  • Travel plan or invitation letter, if you have one

How to apply

  1. Fill in the DS-160 form online and print the confirmation page with the barcode.
  2. Pay the visa fee as shown on the official appointment website.
  3. Book an interview at the US Embassy in Islamabad or the US Consulate General in Karachi.
  4. Gather your documents and attend the interview in person.
  5. Wait for the decision. Some cases need extra administrative processing.

Tips

  • Answer every DS-160 question honestly. Your interview answers must match the form.
  • Apply early, because interview waits and processing times vary.
  • Do not book non-refundable flights before the visa is issued.
  • Be clear and short in the interview, and show real reasons you will return to Pakistan.
