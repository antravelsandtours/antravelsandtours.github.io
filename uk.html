UK visitor visa from Pakistan

How to apply for a UK Standard Visitor visa for tourism, family visits or short business trips.

Who needs it

Pakistani passport holders need a visa before travelling to the UK as a visitor. The Standard Visitor visa is usually for stays of up to 6 months, and you cannot work or study long term on it.

Documents you usually need

  • Valid passport and any old passports with travel history
  • Recent bank statements and proof of income
  • Employment letter, business papers or proof of studies
  • Travel plan and where you will stay
  • Invitation letter and host documents, if visiting family or friends
  • Proof of your ties to Pakistan
  • Any previous visas or travel history

How to apply

  1. Create an account and fill in the online application on the official UK government website.
  2. Pay the visa fee shown during the application.
  3. Book an appointment at the visa application centre to give your fingerprints and photo.
  4. Submit your documents as the website instructs.
  5. Wait for the decision and collect your passport as advised.

Tips

  • Keep your documents recent, clear and consistent with your application.
  • Explain your trip plan simply and honestly.
  • If a document is not in English, include a certified translation.
  • Apply well before your travel date, and avoid paying for flights early.
